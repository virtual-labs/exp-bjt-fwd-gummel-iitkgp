## References
#### Books
1) Ian Getreu, Modeling the Bipolar Transistor, Tektronix, Inc., 1976. (This is a foundational, classic text specifically dedicated to BJT modeling and parameter extraction from Gummel plots and other characteristics.)

2) G. Massobrio and P. Antognetti, Semiconductor Device Modeling with SPICE, 2nd ed., McGraw-Hill, 1993. (Contains a detailed chapter on the BJT Gummel-Poon model and the physical meaning of its parameters, including how they relate to the Gummel plot.)
   
3) S. M. Sze and Kwok K. Ng, Physics of Semiconductor Devices, 3rd ed., Wiley, 2006.
(Provides the deep semiconductor physics background for the collector and base currents ($I_c$, $I_b$) that are measured in a Gummel plot.)
