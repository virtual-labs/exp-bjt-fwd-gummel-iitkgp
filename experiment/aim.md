## Aim of experiment
xtraction of bipolar SPICE Gummel-Poon parameters related to forward Gummel (Ic and Ib vs. Vbe) characteristics

#### The following SPICE parameters are extracted:
- IS, Transport saturation current
- NF, Forward current emission coefficient
- RE, Emitter Resistance
- ISE, B-E leakage saturation current
- NE, B-E leakage emission coefficient
- BF, Ideal maximum forward beta
- BETA_MAX, Maximum forward beta
- IKF, Corner for forward beta high current roll-off
